# Vendor Evaluation Rules

## Evaluation Timing

Evaluate vendors at four points:

1. After first quotation.
2. After order confirmation.
3. After delivery or installation.
4. After project completion or defect response.

## Evaluation Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Actual Supplier Price Satisfaction | Was the real supplier price competitive? |
| Lead Time Satisfaction | Did the vendor meet the promised date? |
| Defect Occurred | Did product or service defects occur? |
| A/S Response Speed | How fast and responsible was the A/S response? |
| Installer Satisfaction | Did the site crew prefer this vendor or product? |
| Client Claim Occurred | Did the vendor cause customer-facing complaints? |
| Repeat Trade Recommended | Should ECOREAN use this vendor again? |

## Scoring Guide

- 90-100: long-term partner candidate
- 75-89: usable preferred vendor
- 60-74: use with caution
- 40-59: one-time or backup only
- 0-39: blacklist candidate

## Long-Term Partner Conditions

A vendor can become a long-term partner when:

- supplier price is consistently competitive
- payment terms support ECOREAN cashflow
- delivery is reliable
- defect response is fast
- A/S responsibility is clear
- installer satisfaction is high
- client claims are rare
- they provide written quotes and evidence

## Blacklist Candidate Conditions

Blacklist candidate when:

- repeated missed delivery
- hidden extra cost
- false quotation
- refusal to handle clear defect
- repeated product defect
- client claim caused by vendor
- payment dispute or tax invoice issue
- unsafe or unreliable installation

## Master DB Feedback

Vendor evaluation can create candidates for:

- supplier price update
- lead time update
- defect risk update
- preferred supplier update
- blacklist update
- payment condition update

Every feedback item requires CEO approval.
