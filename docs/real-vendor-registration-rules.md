# Real Vendor Registration Rules

## Purpose

This document defines how public vendor candidates become real ECOREAN Vendor CRM records.

## Registration States

- `CANDIDATE`: collected from public information.
- `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`: needs phone, KakaoTalk, visit, or quote verification.
- `CONTACTED`: first contact completed.
- `VERIFIED`: business existence, handled items, contact channel, and quote process confirmed.
- `PARTNER_CANDIDATE`: repeat transaction or strong terms confirmed.
- `BLACKLIST_CANDIDATE`: serious risk found.
- `APPROVED_VENDOR`: CEO approved vendor master registration.

## Public Candidate Rule

Public data can be stored only as candidate data.

It cannot be used as final Vendor Master evidence until manually verified.

## No Guessing Rule

Unknown values must remain:

- `UNKNOWN`
- `NEEDS_RESEARCH`
- `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`

Do not estimate price, payment terms, defect response, or trust level.

## Privacy Rule

Do not collect:

- personal mobile phone numbers
- personal email addresses
- private messenger IDs
- resident registration information
- bank account information
- unnecessary representative personal data

Allowed:

- business name
- business landline
- public business address
- official website
- public source URL
- handled items
- handled brands

## Vendor Master Eligibility

A vendor can enter approved Vendor Master only after:

1. Source URL is recorded.
2. Business contact channel is confirmed.
3. Handled item is confirmed.
4. Supplier price or quote process is confirmed.
5. Payment condition is confirmed.
6. Defect/A/S responsibility is checked.
7. CEO approval is recorded.

## Approval Required

CEO approval is required for:

- new vendor registration
- preferred vendor designation
- blacklist registration
- Master DB supplier price reflection
- payment condition changes
- high-value supplier replacement
