# Vendor Data Privacy Rules

## Principle

ECOREAN Vendor CRM stores business relationship data, not unnecessary personal data.

## Do Not Collect

- personal mobile phone numbers
- personal email addresses
- private messenger IDs
- home addresses
- bank account data
- identification numbers
- employee personal records

## Allowed Data

- business name
- representative business landline
- business address
- official website
- public source URL
- handled product categories
- public business hours
- public service area

## Source Reliability

Every vendor candidate must have:

- `sourceUrl`
- `sourceType`
- `collectedAt`
- `dataConfidence`
- `needsManualVerification`

## Verification

A public candidate becomes verified only after phone, KakaoTalk, meeting, or quote confirmation.

## Price Privacy

Supplier price is private business data.

It must be collected through direct contact and stored in Contact Log, not scraped from public websites.
